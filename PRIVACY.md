# Privacy Policy — Read Any Language

_Last updated: 10 July 2026_

Read Any Language ("the extension") converts text you select on a web page into
speech using the ElevenLabs text-to-speech API. This policy explains exactly
what data the extension handles.

## What the extension handles

- **Your ElevenLabs API key.** You enter this in the extension's settings. It is
  stored locally in your browser via `chrome.storage.sync` and is sent only to
  ElevenLabs (`api.elevenlabs.io`) as the authentication header for requests you
  initiate. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.
- **Text you select.** When you highlight text and click the read-aloud button,
  that selected text is sent directly from your browser to ElevenLabs to
  generate the audio. Text you do not select, and other page content, are never
  read or transmitted.
- **Your preferences.** Your chosen voice, model, and per-selection character
  limit are stored locally in your browser.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does not operate any server. **Nothing is ever sent to the developer.**
- It uses no analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising.
- It does not sell or share your data with anyone. The only third party that
  receives any data is ElevenLabs, and only the data required to perform a
  read-aloud action you explicitly request.
- It does not collect personally identifiable information, or health, financial,
  location, or browsing-history data.

## Third-party service

Audio is generated by ElevenLabs. Your selected text and API key are processed
by ElevenLabs under their privacy policy:
<https://elevenlabs.io/privacy>

## Data retention

The extension stores your API key and preferences locally until you remove them
or uninstall the extension. The developer retains no data, because the developer
collects none.
